                5Density calibration optional

                Outline each reference phantom where it is solid and enter its measured density. VoxelBench fits density to the median gray of each phantom, and to air if included. The result is the equivalent density of the phantom material: doped or filled material does not read its true density. Crop the phantoms out of the sample in step 1 if they are as dense as the part; calibration reads them either way. Scan protocol: docs/PHANTOM_PROTOCOL.md in the repository.

                No phantoms? A relative scale needs only air and the part's own fully dense material: 0 = air, 1 = dense. Outline glossy, pore-free material once per material; every gray then also reads as a fraction of dense, in this scan's own units, whatever the scan settings.

                    8Wall thickness optional

                    Local wall thickness of the sample mask (pores filled), at the ridge of the distance to the outside: about 2 × that distance − ½ analysis voxel, ±½ voxel. Walls thinner than about 3 analysis voxels are not reported, so the smallest value shown is 3.5 voxels; corners of the part are not walls. Takes up to about 15 s on a large scan.

                    What this shows

                      93D figure optional

                      The part in 3D for slides and papers: an opaque view with the near quarter cut away, each phase colored (dense and porous, doped and base material, enclosed gas, clumps), and a see-through view with gas red and clumps green. Phases on a relative density scale (air 0, dense base material 1) after a 0.8-voxel smoothing: pore below 0.40, porous 0.40 to 0.85, dense from 0.85. Doped where the dense phase reads above the split or clustered clumps are within 1.5 mm. Enclosed gas: pores outside air does not reach. Needs only the sample mask; drawn on a grid of about 80 µm. Takes up to a minute on a large part.

                      Figure set

                      Samples added here are kept in this browser and drawn together at one scale: a row of cutaways with their numbers, and a turntable film of the see-through views.

                      Scan information

                      Archimedes density (optional)

                      Archimedes porosity is 1 − measured/solid density. A sealed sample keeps water out of every pore, so it compares with CT total porosity; an unsealed one lets water into the open pores, so it compares with CT closed porosity. Solid density: the fully dense material (see the material's datasheet).

                      Orientation scan axes

                      A part scanned at an angle can be leveled: its long axis to x, its thinnest direction to z (up), so depth, sections and wall thickness follow the part. Segment the scan first; the part's axes come from its sample mask.

                      Gray values are uncalibrated (no reference phantoms). Navy: outside the reconstructed field of view (FOV); dashed cyan line: FOV edge. The holder is removed automatically (step 1 > Holder); packaging and reconstruction artifacts are not sample, so crop them out in step 1.
