Segmentation parameters

3Gray-value zones

Segments zones by gray offset: local matrix gray relative to the pore-free ends of the long axis at equal depth below the surface. Gray value reflects bulk attenuation, not composition.

Region labels

Labels are your assignments, not measurements. Deleting a label keeps regions drawn with it; they show as (deleted label).

Relative density from a reference region

Relative density: air 0, reference region 1. g/cm³ scales by the reference density entered; assumes equal mass attenuation for all phases and no beam-hardening correction.

      Dimensions: each Measure (caliper) line with a nominal passes when nominal − tol ≤ measured ≤ nominal + tol. The measured length is between the clicked points on the slice, at the loaded voxel size.

      5Density calibration optional

      Outline each reference phantom where it is solid and enter its measured density. VoxelBench fits density to the median gray of each phantom, and to air if included. The result is the equivalent density of the phantom material: doped or filled material does not read its true density. Crop the phantoms out of the sample in step 1 if they are as dense as the part; calibration reads them either way. Scan protocol: docs/PHANTOM_PROTOCOL.md in the repository.

      6Compare to CAD optional

      Open the part's nominal STL. VoxelBench aligns it to the scanned surface and measures, from each point of the nominal surface along its outward normal, the distance to the scanned surface: positive where the scan has material outside the nominal, negative where material is missing.

      7Filler and fibers optional

      Finds the bright phase inside the sample (filler particles, fibers) after pore segmentation.

      8Wall thickness optional

      Local wall thickness of the sample mask (pores filled), at the ridge of the distance to the outside: about 2 × that distance − ½ analysis voxel, ±½ voxel. Walls under about 2 analysis voxels are not resolved.

      Scan information
